![Logo 01]({{ site.baseurl }}/logos/01.png)


An abstract geometric design built from overlapping circles that form a unified, flower-like symbol. 

The composition centers on a prominent green circle, flanked by teal segments, accented with orange triangular elements, and grounded by deep purple sections at the base. The clean white spaces between each colored segment create visual breathing room while maintaining the logo's cohesive circular structure. 

This modern, minimalist design reflects my approach to technology and problem-solving — where different elements come together to create something greater than the sum of their parts.
